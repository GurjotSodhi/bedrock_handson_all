## What is Amazon Bedrock?


Amazon Bedrock is a fully managed service that makes high-performing foundation models (FMs) available through a unified API. It lets you build and scale generative AI applications without managing any infrastructure.

&nbsp;

## Key concepts

- **Foundation models**: Bedrock gives you access to a choice of models from Amazon (Nova), Anthropic (Claude), Meta (Llama), Mistral, Cohere, and others. You can pick the model that fits your use case for text generation, chat, embeddings, image generation, and more.

- **Serverless**: There are no servers to provision or manage. You make API calls and pay for what you use.

- **Single API**: The [Converse API](/bedrock/foundation/converse-api/) provides a consistent interface across text models, so you can switch between them without rewriting your application code.

- **Customization**: You can fine-tune models with your own data or use Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) via Knowledge Bases to ground responses in your documents.
